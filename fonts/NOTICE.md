# HanCue font credits and licensing

HanCue adds Latin syllable cues on top of Nanum Myeongjo, Nanum Gothic,
and Nanum Pen Script. The original Korean outlines are preserved.
Project created by [@yrezgui](https://github.com/yrezgui) with AI,
inspired by [Furifont](https://furifont.antfu.me/).
